# SoapLedger

Operational software for managing a soap business from order capture through fulfilment.

## What it does
- Captures and manages customer orders
- Prints shipping and product labels
- Supports fulfilment workflows
- Provides a revenue dashboard for operational visibility

## Why I built it
I built SoapLedger to reduce manual operational work and give the business a clearer view of orders, shipping, labels, and revenue in one place.
